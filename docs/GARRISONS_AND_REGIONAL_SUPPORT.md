# Garrisons and regional support

Implementation is in progress. Scripts and data have offline checks; this is not a Workbench compile or gameplay acceptance report.

Focused actual-source regressions cover region membership, support normalization, registry caches, game-clock timing, deployment expiry, queue-source cancellation, resource failures, orphaned crew, logical asset rebinding and serializer round trips/recovery. Native services are mocked; these do not replace Workbench compilation, queue cancellation, multiplayer or save/load acceptance.

Towns and cities have no flag interaction. Unused Town_Flag/City_Flag resources and the settlement-flag switch/API are removed. MilitaryOutpost is replaced by MilitaryBase; Outpost and MilitaryBase directly inherit Location_Base with their own flags/garrisons. All 41 stable campaign IDs remain. Extenders must update removed resource references and renamed component attributes.

## Regional support

As agreed with Jasper, each military site joins its nearest town/city's region. Distance is horizontal; stable IDs break ties. `m_sSupportRegionId` on a location overrides this assignment; several settlements can share a region ID. For generated Chicken layers, edit the CSV `RegionId` column before regeneration (blank uses the automatic assignment). It is scenario data, not a saved campaign ownership map.

Regional support is opt-in per scenario: the script default and shared game-mode prefab disable it. Only Chicken explicitly enables the current experimental policy. Existing worlds inheriting the shared prefab retain their support unless they opt in or another support authority changes it. The drift rate and policy remain provisional balancing choices.

When enabled, every 60 seconds after campaign setup, count the region's flag-capturable installations by controlling role. Their equal shares define the target support distribution. Move each settlement toward that target by at most two percentage points for any role per cycle. Interpolating all three values together keeps their sum at 100 without granting support to an absent role. A region with no installations has no military influence and retains support. The original initial support, >50% ownership rule, fixed control and flip cooldown remain authoritative. From 0% Resistance support, complete regional military control takes 26 default cycles to exceed 50%; a 50/50 split does not itself produce a majority.

Regional timing/step are attributes on AROR_RegionalSupportComponent. Region membership and settlement references are computed once and invalidated when locations register or disappear. Ownership remains dynamic and is counted each support cycle. This supplies ongoing military influence through the existing support authority; no new support save section is added.

## Defence recipes

| Location | Default recipe |
| --- | --- |
| Ordinary outpost | 8 infantry, 1 crewed MG; no vehicles even under a stronger infantry override |
| Military outpost/base | 24 infantry, 3 crewed MGs, 1 parked unmanned LAV/BTR, 1 random crewed vehicle |
| Resources / factory / port | Roll level 1–3 once: 8/16/24 infantry, 1/2/3 MGs, 0/1/1 crewed vehicles; level 3 also has parked armour |
| Airbase | 32 infantry, 4 MGs, parked armour, 3 random crewed vehicles |

All recipes use four-person infantry teams. Crew is additional and comes from the actual non-cargo compartments. Register the group's vehicle before requesting seats and require observed occupancy. Continue checking seats after readiness, retrying nearby living assigned AI at a configurable cadence; players, foreign actors and destroyed hulls are excluded. Mounted crews receive no infantry defend waypoint; infantry and surviving crew from lost hulls retain on-foot defence. Parked armour remains intentionally unmanned. Occupier random hulls are LAV-25/M2 Humvee; Invaders use BTR-70/BRDM-2 with equivalent parked BTR and NSV roles. Each role's assets and native faction are configured independently in `AROR_GarrisonFaction`, checked against the shared role mapping. This does not enable Invader QRF admission; garrisons follow the actual site controller and their own valid binding.

These are provisional strengths. Fresh four-person teams use the configured rifleman plus optional automatic-rifleman, grenadier and leader variants. Variants bind through the existing logical roster schema; old rifle-only saves remain unchanged, and missing specialist configuration falls back to the rifleman. Some teams hold sectors while alternate teams periodically move between home and interior defence goals or react to recent validated contact. Goals remain local and walkable; static/vehicle crews keep their mounted tasks. Building-interior occupation and runtime fortification construction remain unimplemented.

Eleven military compositions provide editable defence pads and optional walls. The user's current ordinary-outpost prefab intentionally has no generated sandbag walls, and Chotain has a hand-authored castle. Earlier native footprint passes predate those edits. Preserve the saved geometry and run a fresh editor audit; catalogue regeneration is not safe until authored-subtree preservation and pose reconciliation are complete. The read-only [saved-scene report](CAMPAIGN_SITE_READINESS.csv) covers all 23 military roots without replacing their geometry.

## Activation, losses and capture

The manager caches location/garrison/game-flow references and takes one player snapshot per configured 1–2 second proximity scan (default 1 second). Registration/deletion invalidates the cache. Construction remains limited to one hull or team request per 250 ms globally. Teleports are noticed by the next proximity scan. Retirement and queue/deployment deadlines use elapsed game time, not callback counts. The 1km activation, 6m local clearance (3m crew staging) and separate 96-personnel default cap remain configurable.

Earlier Levie Play tests identified terrain and blocked-pad problems; later native tests confirmed garrison deployment and the clear/capture/QRF sequence, including relocated East Resources. These results predate the PR-review lifecycle and migration corrections.

Deployment is pending for a bounded window (default 60 seconds). Capacity accounting covers submitted queues and physical assets instead of reserving every soldier in a virtual recipe. Nearby sites take turns receiving complete teams before optional heavy assets; the same shared cap remains authoritative. An empty capacity-blocked site can wait up to 180 additional seconds. Once a capacity-limited site has physical defenders and its submitted queues resolve, it closes remaining producers so freeing capacity during clearance cannot create late reinforcements. Resource/faction/crew/pad failures retain their bounded retries. Each submitted queue has its own timeout (default 30 seconds); its source group is stopped and replaced only for existing survivors. Missing slots are omitted for that activation without recording casualties. Closing the window stops all remaining producers before capture unlocks. Living hostile checks continue to block capture. Native queue/player-transfer safety still requires acceptance.

Full-footprint terrain failures normally defer the site. Chicken explicitly enables `m_bInfantryFallback`: unspawned heavy assets and their attached crews are omitted for that activation, while infantry and orphaned survivors can use individually validated pads. Land, slope, collision, soldier navigation, player clearance and the canonical capture area remain mandatory. Local repair searches are bounded to 25 candidates; the fallback does not approve unsafe walls or vehicle access.

Chicken's QRF sequence is **clear the outpost, capture the flag, then request the QRF**. `m_bCombatQRFs` defaults off and is explicitly off in Chicken: hostile damage does not dispatch a response. Validated reports can still update bounded crackdown and recent contact for local defenders and retirement safety. Enabled canonical captures queue the previous hostile owner's response, subject to cooldowns, capacity and a safe friendly land origin. Chicken also opts into the minimum level-1 capture response from the readiness-appropriate equipment pool when no funded recipe is affordable; overhead and confirmed losses remain payable through Economy/debt. Settlement support flips retain their separately enabled capture-response path. Extenders can explicitly re-enable combat responses. Existing operations from a previous Play session are not retroactively cancelled.

Retirement defaults to 2,500 m and 120 continuous seconds. When another site waits for personnel capacity, a garrison without recent combat may retire after 15 continuous seconds beyond 1,200 m, always outside the activation radius. Every player, including spectators with a controlled entity or dead characters, must be far enough from both site and tracked actors before deletion. Returning resets the grace timer. Native player control and foreign vehicle occupants prevent cleanup ownership from being taken away. Explicitly transferred assets become unavailable roster slots; moving a remaining hull outside the site also prevents its later recreation. Group queues are cancelled during retirement, and living administrative retirement is not counted as a death.

Casualties and coarse health survive retirement. Schema 3 saves logical kind/catalogue variant, group/seat relationships and loss/health flags, with no prefab paths or transforms. Versions 1/2 migrate to current role assets and freshly validated pads. A damaged slot is conservatively withheld without rejecting its neighbours; an unreadable header quarantines the section and prevents a free fresh roster. Extra seats introduced by a changed catalogue do not award replacement crew. Surviving crew whose hull was lost re-enter on foot. Variants currently identify configured catalogue positions; stable catalogue IDs remain part of future faction work.

Ownership changes close deployment queues and clear QRF assignments. Existing actors retire safely before a new enemy owner gets a fresh roster; Resistance receives no automatic garrison. Initial rosters still have no cost and casualties are not replenished: that interim policy is unchanged pending an agreed resource-backed replenishment contract with the economy owner. No funds, manpower or stock authority is added here.
